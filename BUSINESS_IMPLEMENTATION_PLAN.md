## 💳 Payment & Non-Custodial Architecture

### Supported Gateways & Networks:
1. **Direct Blockchain Monitoring**:
   - User transfers USDT/USDC to a designated multi-chain deposit address.
   - Python backend verifies the transaction hash on TronGrid, BSCScan, PolygonScan, or Solscan API.
   - When verified $\rightarrow$ Bot automatically issues a single-use 24-hour expiring Telegram VIP invite link.
2. **Crypto Payment Gateways (Optional Automated Webhooks)**:
   - OxaPay, Cryptomus, or NOWPayments (0.4%–1% processing fee).
   - Direct automatic settlement into cold storage (Ledger, TrustWallet, Phantom).
